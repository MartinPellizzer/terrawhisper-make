# L. One essential architectural decision

I would **not** make your SQL database a direct copy of WFO or POWO.

Instead, preserve:

- Your own internal plant identifier.
- External taxonomic identifiers from each source.
- The source-specific accepted name.
- Taxonomic classification as reported by each source.
- The date and version of each imported dataset.
- Original publication references.
- Evidence supporting taxonomic decisions.
- Your editorial decision about which taxonomic treatment to display.

This is particularly important because WFO, COL, POWO, and GBIF may differ in accepted names, ranks, or taxonomic circumscriptions.

Your database should preserve those differences rather than silently erase them.
